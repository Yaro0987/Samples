This Cookie Policy explains how Deep Design Gym uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us understand how you use our site and improve your experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly (e.g., shopping cart, login sessions).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences (e.g., dark mode toggle, language).
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign performance.

3. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may allow third-party services (e.g., Google Analytics, social media widgets) to set cookies on your device for analytics and marketing purposes.
